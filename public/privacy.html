Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version: we only collect what's needed to run your reading journal. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. You can delete your account, and everything in it, at any time from the account menu.

1. Who we are

MyBookShelf is a personal reading journal. It is a small, independently run project, not a company. In this policy, "MyBookShelf", "we" and "us" mean the people who run it, and "you" means anyone who uses it.

We handle personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules. For any privacy question or request, email mapleartsandcrafts@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: the name and email address you sign up with, and your password. We never store your password itself, only a one-way scrambled version (a bcrypt hash) that can't be turned back into it.
  • Your reading journal: the books you add and everything you record about them, such as title, author, summary, genres, format, status, page progress, ratings, spice level, quotes, reviews, notes, start and finish dates, favourites, and your reading goals.
  • Cover images you upload.
  • Password reset requests: when you ask to reset your password, we keep a scrambled copy of the reset link's code until it is used or expires (after one hour).
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log things such as your IP address, browser type and the time of each request, to keep the service running and secure.

We do not collect payment details, location, contacts, or anything from your device beyond what's listed here.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. It holds a secure login token that expires after one day, can't be read by scripts on the page, and is removed when you log out. We also keep your name, email and chosen theme in your browser's local storage so pages show the right details and colours straight away. We don't use cookies for tracking or advertising, and there are no analytics or ad scripts on the site. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store and show your shelf, reading progress, statistics and goals.
  • To send you emails you ask for, such as password reset links.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by signing you out everywhere after a password reset.

We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or read your journal for any purpose other than fixing a problem you've asked us to help with.

5. Services we rely on

MyBookShelf runs on a few trusted providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Vercel: hosts the website and app, and stores the cover images you upload (Vercel Blob).
  • Neon: stores the database with your account and journal.
  • Google Books: when you search for a book to add, the search text is sent to Google Books to find matching titles, covers and summaries. Your name and email are not sent with it.
  • Google (Gmail): sends our emails, such as password reset links.

These providers may store data on servers outside the Philippines. By using MyBookShelf, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries, with protections at least as strong as those required by Philippine law wherever we can arrange it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and journal for as long as your account exists. Password reset codes are deleted once used or replaced. When you delete your account, your profile, books, notes, reviews, quotes, reading history, goals and uploaded covers are permanently deleted straight away. Our providers may keep backup copies for a short period before they are overwritten.

7. How we protect it

The site is served over HTTPS, passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes, reset links expire after one hour and work once, and resetting your password signs you out on every device. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password you don't use elsewhere. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will notify you and, where required, the National Privacy Commission.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is collected and used (this page).
  • Access your data. Most of it is visible in the app; email us for a full copy.
  • Correct it. You can edit your books at any time; email us to change your name or email.
  • Erase or block it. Use Delete account in the account menu, or email us.
  • Object to processing, and withdraw consent, by deleting your account.
  • Data portability: ask us and we'll send your journal in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Be compensated for damages caused by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or misused data.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

We'll reply to requests sent by email within 15 days.

9. Children

MyBookShelf is meant for general audiences. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information without that permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we'll let you know by email or in the app before it takes effect.

11. Contact us

Questions, requests or concerns: mapleartsandcrafts@gmail.com.
